02 — Good Operations

WHAT DOES “GOOD OPERATIONS” ACTUALLY MEAN?

Operations is not simply about keeping things running. It is about designing, building and operating services with reliability in mind — while continuously learning from what happens in production.

01

RELIABLE

Services you can trust.
Services behave as expected, consistently and predictably.

02

RESILIENT

Services that recover.
When something fails, the organization can detect, respond and recover.

03

CONTINUOUSLY IMPROVING

Operations that learn.
Every incident, signal and experience creates an opportunity to improve.

RELIABILITY IS NOT A METRIC.
IT IS A DESIGN CHOICE.
03 — The Operations Loop

OPERATIONS IS NOT A PHASE.

OPERATIONS IS A LIFECYCLE.

Every stage influences the next. Design for operability, build for reliability, run with discipline, learn from reality and improve the system.

04 — Operations × Industry

TWO OPERATIONAL WORLDS. ONE SHARED DISCIPLINE.

Industrial Operations and IT Operations operate different systems, but the fundamentals are familiar: detect, understand, recover, learn and improve.

WHAT CAN IT OPERATIONS LEARN FROM INDUSTRY?
Industrial environments have been solving problems of reliability, maintenance, failure, recovery and continuous improvement for decades. The technologies differ. The operational questions often do not.

08 — About

I WORK AT THE INTERSECTION OF PRODUCT, OPERATIONS AND INDUSTRY.

Who is behind Operations Lab?

I'm Vincent.

I work at the intersection of Product Management, IT Operations and Service Management, in a large industrial environment.

My work sits where technology meets reality: services have to run, people have to collaborate, problems have to be solved and systems have to continuously improve.

Working in an industrial company has shaped the way I think about Operations. Industrial environments have been dealing with reliability, maintenance, failure, recovery and continuous improvement for decades. IT Operations faces many of the same fundamental challenges — in a different environment.

Operations Lab is where I explore these connections: a place to share ideas, frameworks, experiments and lessons learned along the way.
