04 — MY APPROACH

FROM
INCIDENTS
TO SYSTEMS.

An incident is an event. The operating system around it is the real subject.

I am interested in the mechanisms behind performance: signals, roles, standards, decision paths, service design, feedback loops and the conditions that allow teams to perform consistently.

The goal is not to eliminate every failure. It is to create systems that detect earlier, recover faster and learn from what happened.
